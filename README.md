## About

Why I made this repo? Simply, lately I do a lot of transit between Jakarta and Bandung, and every trip starts with the same long overthinking session.

My weeks are a mix of short hops across Jakarta and the occasional ride out to Bandung and back. Every one of these trips has too many ways to do it, and every option lives in a different app, so I end up opening five tabs just to compare one trip.

So I'm building Kelana, a place where I just say _"I want to go from A to B"_ and it builds the whole trip for me, door to door. Walk or ride to the station, the train or bus in between, and the ride to my last destination.

_Kelana_ itself means **wanderer** in Indonesian, which is basically me every weekend.
